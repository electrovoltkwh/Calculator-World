📘 GPA Formula

GPA is calculated using credit-weighted grade points.

GPA = Σ(Credit × Grade Point) ÷ Σ Credits

📊 CGPA Formula

CGPA uses semester GPA weighted according to semester credits.

CGPA = Σ(GPA × Credits) ÷ Σ Credits

⚠️ Important

Different universities may use different grade mappings and percentage conversion rules.
