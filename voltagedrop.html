📐 Formula

DC / 1-Phase:
Vd = I × R

3-Phase:
Vd = √3 × I × R

Voltage Drop %:
(Vd / V) × 100
REFERENCE

📚 Notes

DC: Uses the complete circuit loop resistance.

1-Phase AC: Uses the approximate two-conductor path.

3-Phase AC: Uses √3 × I × R.

The calculation uses cable resistivity and cross-sectional area. Actual installations can also be affected by temperature, reactance, installation method and conductor configuration.
